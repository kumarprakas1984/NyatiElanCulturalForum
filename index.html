Nyati Elan Cultural Forum - Residents Portal

Welcome to the internal portal for managing and sharing community contributions data for the Nyati Elan Cultural Forum, located in Wagholi, Pune, Maharashtra.

About the Application

This application is a secure, internal tool designed exclusively for authorized volunteers and society residents. It simplifies the process of recording and tracking resident contributions, ensuring our records are always accurate and up-to-date. All data is managed directly within our community's own Google Sheet.

Login

Key Features

  • Secure Google Sign-In: Only authorized volunteers can see the admin dashboard to record the contribution or upload photos, other residents can access the portal using their Google account to check contribution and expense details.
  • Centralized Data Management: View and update contribution details for all residents in real-time.
  • Efficient Workflow: Easily navigate through buildings and flats to find and update resident information.
  • Automated Record Keeping: Automatically records the date, time, and the volunteer's email for each transaction, ensuring transparency.
  • Instant WhatsApp Receipts: Send payment confirmation receipts to residents via WhatsApp with a single click.
  • Community Photo Uploads: Upload photos from community events directly to our shared Google Drive folder.
