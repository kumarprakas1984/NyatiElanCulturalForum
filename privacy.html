Privacy Policy for Nyati Elan Cultural Forum - Admin Portal

Effective Date: August 25, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Nyati Elan Cultural Forum - Admin Portal ("the Application") collects, uses, and handles information when you, an authorized volunteer, use our application.

1. Information We Collect

This application is an internal tool and does not store user data on its own servers. All data is read from and written directly to the Nyati Elan Cultural Forum's own Google Workspace account (Google Sheets and Google Drive).

  • Information from Google Sign-In: When you log in to the Application using your Google account, we access basic profile information provided by Google. This includes:
    • Name and Email Address: We use your name to display a personalized greeting and your email address to record who performed a transaction in the "Recorded By" column of our Google Sheet. This provides an essential audit trail.
  • Data Processed Through Google APIs:
    • Google Sheets: The Application requests permission to read and write to a specific Google Sheet owned by the Nyati Elan Cultural Forum. This sheet contains community contribution data, including resident names, flat numbers, contact details, payment status, amounts, and comments. The Application does NOT have access to any other files or spreadsheets in your Google account.
    • Google Drive: The Application requests permission to upload files to a specific, pre-configured Google Drive folder owned by the Nyati Elan Cultural Forum. This is used solely for uploading community event photos. The Application CANNOT read, list, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information accessed is used exclusively for the following internal administrative purposes:

  • To authenticate you as an authorized volunteer.
  • To read and display resident contribution data from our designated Google Sheet.
  • To update or add new contribution records to the same Google Sheet.
  • To provide an audit trail by logging the logged-in volunteer's email with each transaction.
  • To pre-fill a resident's phone number and a receipt message for sending via WhatsApp.
  • To upload community photos to the designated Google Drive folder.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

The Application itself is a stateless web client and does not store any personal data. All data persists solely within the Nyati Elan Cultural Forum's own Google Sheet and Google Drive. We do not share any information with third parties.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The Application's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only as described above to provide the core functionality of this internal administrative tool.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
